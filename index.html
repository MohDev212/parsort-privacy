Parsort privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Parsort is a puzzle game by MohDev Games. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What the game stores

Your scores, streak and level progress are stored on your device. The game has no accounts, and nothing you do in it is linked to your name.

Analytics and crash reports

Parsort uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to fix problems. They collect device and app identifiers, approximate location from your IP address, how you use the game (such as holes started and finished), and crash and performance data. We use this only to improve the game, and it's retained and handled by Google. See Firebase privacy information.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, your device schedules it locally. No reminder data leaves your device, and you can turn it off in the game's settings.

Advertising

Parsort shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device identifiers, including the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. Google uses this to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We don't receive your payment details.

Children

Parsort is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device. You can reset your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
